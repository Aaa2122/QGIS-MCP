# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

QGIS Agent MCP is committed to a welcoming, respectful and harassment-free community. We welcome participation regardless of experience, identity, background, disability, appearance, nationality, religion or technical preference.

## Expected behaviour

Community members are expected to:

- communicate with empathy, patience and respect;
- discuss ideas and code without attacking people;
- give actionable feedback and accept constructive correction;
- respect privacy, credentials, project data and responsible-disclosure boundaries;
- make space for newcomers and different levels of QGIS or MCP experience;
- disclose conflicts of interest when they affect a recommendation or review.

Examples of unacceptable behaviour include harassment, discrimination, sexualized attention, threats, doxxing, deliberate intimidation, sustained disruption, publishing private information, or retaliating against someone who reports a concern.

## Scope

This code applies in the repository, Issues, Pull Requests, Discussions, release channels and other spaces where someone represents the project. It also applies to private communication when that conduct materially affects community safety.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to **96547921+Aaa2122@users.noreply.github.com**. Include links or screenshots when safe to do so. Do not open a public issue for sensitive reports.

Maintainers will review reports promptly, minimize unnecessary disclosure and act proportionally. Possible responses include a private warning, content removal, temporary participation restrictions or a permanent ban. Maintainers who are directly involved in a report must recuse themselves from deciding its outcome whenever another neutral reviewer is available.

Good-faith reports will not result in retaliation. Knowingly false or malicious reports may themselves violate this code.
